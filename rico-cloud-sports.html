Common Questions

Everything you
need to know.

What sports does this work for?+
Any sport where a coach or facility is selling lessons, programs, camps, or clinics. The system is built around the athlete/parent relationship and the coaching business model — not a specific sport. Baseball, basketball, soccer, tennis, swimming, gymnastics, martial arts, football, volleyball — they all work with the same architecture.
How does the 7-week reactivation sequence work?+
When an athlete hasn't booked in a set number of days (you configure the trigger), they're automatically enrolled in the 7-week reactivation sequence. It combines SMS and email across 7 weeks — using seasonal timing, performance-focused copy, and comeback offers to get them rebooked without you having to manually reach out.
Can I use this for camps and showcase events?+
Yes. The camp and event registration system is built specifically for sports businesses running camps, clinics, showcases, and tournaments. It handles the registration page, confirmation sequence, pre-event logistics emails, day-of details, and post-event follow-up — all automated.
What does the AI conversation do exactly?+
The AI conversation feature responds to inbound text messages from athletes and parents when you're not available — answering common questions about pricing, scheduling, availability, and next steps. It's configured with your specific business information during setup so every response is accurate and on-brand.
I coach at a facility I don't own. Can this still work for me?+
Yes. The system is deployed to your Rico Cloud account — not the facility's. You own your athlete database, your automations, and your follow-up system. Wherever you coach, your business infrastructure comes with you.
