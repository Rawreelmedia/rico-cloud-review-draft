Common Questions

Everything you
need to know.

What exactly is included in the $1,000 System Deployment Fee?+
The $1,000 covers the complete deployment of your industry snapshot into your Rico Cloud account. That includes all workflows, pipelines, forms, funnels, tags, sequences, and custom values — fully configured with your business name, branding, and details. Onboarding is not included in the deployment fee — it is available separately as a DIY course ($100) or Premium done-for-you onboarding ($500).
What is the $150/mo base subscription for?+
The $150/mo is your Rico Cloud platform subscription — this is what powers the automation engine, CRM, SMS, email, and all the tools your snapshot runs on. It's required because the snapshot lives inside Rico Cloud. Think of it as your platform fee, separate from the System Deployment Fee cost.
How long does deployment actually take?+
7 days from when we receive your completed intake form. We send you the intake form immediately after purchase. Once we have your business details, our team builds and deploys your system within 7 business days. You then receive access and can immediately select your onboarding tier to get up and running.
Do I need to know how to use Rico Cloud before getting a snapshot?+
No. That's the whole point. The snapshot is designed for business owners, not technologists. We handle the entire deployment. After go-live, you choose your onboarding path — a self-paced DIY course ($100) or Premium onboarding ($500) with a 2-hour done-for-you walkthrough. Most clients are fully operational within their first week.
Can I customize the system after it's deployed?+
Yes, completely. Once your snapshot is deployed, you own it. You can edit any workflow, update any copy, add new automations, or adjust any sequence. We also offer ongoing support and optional upgrade packages if you want us to make changes for you.
What if my industry isn't listed?+
Book a discovery call. We build custom snapshots for industries not in our current library. The discovery call lets us scope your specific needs and give you a quote for a custom build.
