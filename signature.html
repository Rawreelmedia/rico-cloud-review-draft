Common Questions

Everything you
need to know.

How does the AI voice actually work?+
AI voice is a trained voice agent connected to your business phone number. When a call comes in and you don't answer (or during after-hours), the AI picks up in a natural voice. It's trained on your business information — services, pricing, FAQs, availability — and can book appointments directly to your calendar, answer common questions, qualify leads, and take detailed messages. You configure what it knows and how it responds.
What's the AI voice usage cost?+
AI voice is $0.15/min — significantly less than what it would cost to have a human answering service. A typical call is 2–4 minutes. If the AI handles 50 calls per month at 3 minutes each, that's $22.50 in usage on top of your $750/mo subscription. Most businesses find it pays for itself with one recovered lead.
Can the AI conversation be trained on my specific business?+
Yes. You configure the AI with your business name, services, pricing, FAQs, hours, and policies. The AI responds based on what you've given it. For questions it can't answer, it escalates to a human team member or collects the contact info for follow-up.
What are Communities?+
Communities let you build a private, branded community inside your Rico Cloud platform. Think of it like a Facebook Group but on your own platform — your clients, students, or members can post, interact, and engage without leaving your ecosystem. Useful for coaching programs, memberships, and customer loyalty.
Is the full CRM included or is that extra?+
Full CRM is included in Pro at no extra cost — lead management, pipeline management, contact management, deal tracking, and opportunity reporting are all part of the plan.
Can I upgrade to Pro from any other plan?+
Yes. Upgrading from any tier to Pro is instant. All your existing data — contacts, websites, workflows, courses, everything — carries over. Pro activates AI voice, AI conversation, Communities, and the full CRM on top of what you already had.
