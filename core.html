Common Questions

Everything you
need to know.

What does "missed call text-back" actually do?+
The moment someone calls your business and you don't answer, Rico Cloud automatically sends them a text message within seconds — "Hey, we missed your call. We'll be right with you!" This recovers leads who would have called your competitor next. You can customize the message.
How does call tracking work?+
Call tracking assigns unique phone numbers to different marketing campaigns — your Google ad, your Facebook ad, your website. When someone calls, you see exactly which source drove the call. Call recording lets you review conversations and coach your team. Source attribution shows you which marketing actually works.
Do I need technical skills to build automations?+
No. Rico Cloud's workflow builder is visual and drag-and-drop. You pick a trigger (like "new lead submitted a form"), then add actions (send SMS, send email, notify your team). We also provide pre-built automation templates for common use cases during onboarding.
How much will I actually spend on usage costs?+
Most small businesses on Core spend $10–$40/mo on usage on top of their $100 subscription. A typical breakdown: 1 phone number ($2) + 500 SMS/mo ($5) + 200 minutes of calls ($4) + email usage ($3–$10) = $14–$21 in usage costs. The usage dashboard shows real-time spend.
What's the difference between Core and Growth?+
Core gives you automation, SMS, call tracking, and reputation management. Growth ($150/mo) adds courses and gated content (sell knowledge products), a unified messaging inbox (all your DMs, emails, and texts in one place), and analytics dashboards. If you want to sell courses or need a single inbox for all channels — that's Growth.
Can I see reviews from all platforms in one place?+
Yes. Core's reputation management dashboard shows Google, Yelp, and Trustpilot reviews in one feed. You can reply to reviews directly from the dashboard without logging into each platform separately.
