Common Questions

Everything you
need to know.

Do I get both the 1A and 1B variants?+
Yes. Both the Performance Training (1A) and Physical Therapy (1B) variants are included in your $1,000 System Deployment Fee. During your intake form, you tell us which one is your primary focus and we deploy accordingly — but both are available in your account.
I'm not technical. Will I be able to use this?+
Yes. You don't build anything — we build it for you. Once your system is deployed, you can choose a DIY Onboarding course ($100) to learn the platform at your own pace, or upgrade to Premium Onboarding ($500) for a done-for-you walkthrough with a 2-hour live call. Either way, onboarding is available the moment your system goes live.
What do I need to provide during intake?+
Your business name, logo, address, phone number, website URL, and your booking link if you have one. We'll also ask about your primary services and which client pain points you want to target. The intake form takes about 15 minutes to complete.
What's the $150/mo subscription for?+
The $150/mo is your Rico Cloud platform subscription — it powers the CRM, automations, SMS, email, and all the tools the snapshot runs on. It's the engine under the hood. The $1,000 is the System Deployment Fee — the cost to build and deploy the system into your account.
Can I edit the copy and workflows after deployment?+
Yes, completely. Once deployed, you own the system. You can edit any message, adjust any workflow, or add new automations whenever you like. The system is yours — we just do the initial heavy lifting.
I already have a Rico Cloud account. Can I still get the snapshot?+
Yes. We can deploy the Coach In a Box snapshot into an existing Rico Cloud account. The $1,000 System Deployment Fee still applies — the deployment work is the same. The $150/mo applies if your current subscription is below the Base tier.
