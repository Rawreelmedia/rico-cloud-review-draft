Common Questions

Everything you
need to know.

How is email marketing different from what I have now?+
If you're using Mailchimp or a similar tool, your email list and your website/booking system are completely separate. With Rico Cloud Basic, every contact — whether they booked an appointment or filled out a form — is in the same database. You can email them instantly without any import/export.
Can I have different booking pages for each service?+
Yes. You get unlimited booking pages, so you can have one for each service type, team member, or location. A salon could have separate pages for haircuts, color, and blowouts — each with its own availability and confirmation workflow.
What does $0.002/email actually cost?+
Very little. 1,000 emails = $2.00. 5,000 emails = $10.00. 10,000 emails = $20.00. This is billed separately from your subscription at the end of each month based on actual sends. Most small businesses spend $2–$15/mo on email usage.
Can I upgrade from Starter to Basic without losing anything?+
Yes. Upgrading is instant. Your websites, contacts, and forms all carry over. The moment you upgrade, booking pages and email marketing are activated in your account.
Do I need to migrate my existing email list?+
You can import your existing email list as a CSV. We'll walk you through this during onboarding. Your contacts will be live and segmentable within minutes.
What's the difference between Basic and Core?+
Basic handles your website, booking, and email marketing. Core ($100/mo) adds automation workflows, 2-way SMS messaging, call tracking, reputation management, and a web chat widget. If you want your business to respond automatically to missed calls, send appointment reminders by text, and request Google reviews — that's Core.
