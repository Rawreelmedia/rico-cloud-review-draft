Usage FAQ

Common Questions.

When am I billed for usage costs?
Usage costs are billed separately from your subscription at the end of each billing cycle. You'll see a usage invoice for the previous month's consumption.
Are there usage caps or alerts?
There are no hard usage caps — your system keeps running. We recommend setting usage alerts in your account dashboard to monitor spend. Alerts can be configured to notify you at any threshold you choose.
Why is AI voice priced at $0.15/min?
AI voice connects to a live phone network and runs real-time AI inference for every second of the call. The $0.15/min rate reflects telephony carrier costs plus AI processing. Most businesses see strong ROI given that AI voice handles inbound calls 24/7 — including nights and weekends — without any human intervention.
Do I need a higher tier just to get started?
No. Starter at $20/mo is a fully functional tier for website hosting, funnels, and forms. You can start there and upgrade to any tier at any time — with no penalty or lock-in. Your data and settings transfer instantly.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

Can I switch plans?
Yes — upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect immediately. There are no penalties or lock-in periods. Your data and settings are preserved across all tier changes.
Is there a contract?
No. All CRM subscriptions are month-to-month. Annual plans save money but are not required. Cancel anytime — your account remains active until the end of the billing period.
What exactly are usage costs?
Usage costs are separate from your subscription and are pay-as-you-go. They apply to telephony (phone numbers, SMS, calls), email sending, premium workflow actions, and AI voice. You only pay for what you use. See the full breakdown in the Usage Costs section above.
Can I buy a domain through Rico Cloud?
Yes. Domain purchases are available at market rate directly through your account. All tiers support custom domains.
Does the annual plan auto-renew?
Yes, annual plans renew automatically at the end of the year. You can cancel or change your plan before the renewal date from within your account settings.
What is unified messaging?
Unified messaging (available at Growth and above) consolidates all your conversations — Facebook DMs, Instagram DMs, email, SMS, and web chat — into a single inbox. No more switching between platforms to respond to leads and clients.
What does AI voice do?
AI voice (Pro tier only) connects a real phone number to a 24/7 AI agent that answers inbound calls, books appointments, answers FAQs, and qualifies leads — all without human intervention. It operates around the clock including nights, weekends, and holidays.
Do I need a higher tier to get started?
No. Starter at $20/mo includes a fully functional website builder, unlimited funnels, and forms. You can start there and upgrade any time with no penalties. Most growing businesses migrate to Basic or Core once they're ready to add automations and messaging.
