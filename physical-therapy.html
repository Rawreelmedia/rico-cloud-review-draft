Common Questions

Everything you
need to know.

Is this different from Coach In a Box?+
Yes. The Physical Therapist & Rehab System is built specifically for clinic-based physical therapy — it includes HIPAA-awareness in its messaging approach, post-discharge protocols, insurance-adjacent copy, and a patient journey focus rather than a performance/sales focus. Coach In a Box Variant 1B shares some architecture but this is the expanded, standalone physical therapist-specific deployment.
How do the pain-specific sequences work?+
When a patient fills out a pain-specific form (e.g., the "Back Pain" funnel page), the system tags them automatically and enrolls them in the corresponding sequence. Every SMS and email in that sequence is written for that specific injury — the messaging is relevant from message one rather than generic.
What happens after a patient is discharged?+
The system triggers three things automatically at discharge: a review request sequence (Google and Healthgrades), a post-discharge check-in sequence at 30, 60, and 90 days, and enrollment into the 360-day long-term nurture. That 360-day nurture runs for a full year — so when a patient's injury flares up 8 months later, they call you first.
Is this HIPAA compliant?+
Rico Cloud can be configured for HIPAA compliance at the platform level. The Physical Therapist system's messaging is designed to be general health and wellness content rather than PHI — it doesn't transmit patient medical records or treatment details via SMS or email. We recommend discussing your specific compliance requirements on the discovery call.
Can I add more pain categories after deployment?+
Yes. The four included categories (Back, Knee, Shoulder, Hip) are the core deployment. Additional pain categories can be added as a separate project after your initial system is live.
