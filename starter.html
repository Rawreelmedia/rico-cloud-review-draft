Common Questions

Everything you
need to know.

Is there a contract or commitment?+
No contract. Month-to-month. Cancel anytime from your dashboard. Annual billing saves you 2 months but is never required.
What happens when I outgrow Starter?+
Upgrade anytime — no data migration, no starting over. Your websites, forms, and contacts come with you. Basic ($50/mo) adds booking and email marketing. Core ($100/mo) adds SMS and automation.
Do I need to know how to code?+
Not at all. Rico Cloud is fully drag-and-drop. If you can type and click, you can build a professional website. We also provide onboarding support for new accounts.
Is hosting included in the $20/mo?+
Yes. Website hosting is included in your subscription. Your domain is purchased separately at market rate, but hosting, SSL, and CDN delivery are all included in your plan.
How many websites can I build?+
Unlimited. No page limits, no funnel limits, no website limits on the Starter plan. Build as many landing pages, websites, and funnels as your business needs.
What's the difference between Starter and Basic?+
Starter is your digital foundation — website, funnels, forms, and contact storage. Basic ($50/mo) adds unlimited calendar booking (replaces Calendly) and email marketing. When you need to book appointments or send emails to your list, Basic is next.
Can I try before I buy?+
Book a free 30-minute demo and we'll walk you through the platform live before you commit to anything.
