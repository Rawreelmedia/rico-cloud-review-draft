Common Questions

Everything you
need to know.

Do all specialties cost the same?+
Yes. All seven specialty variants — Primary Care, Dermatology, Medical Spa, Ophthalmology, Optometry, Orthopedic, and Sports Medicine — are deployed at the same $1,000 System Deployment Fee + $150/mo base subscription. No specialty is priced differently.
What if I offer more than one specialty?+
Book a discovery call. Multi-specialty practices require a scoping conversation to determine how many workflows and pipelines are needed. We'll walk through your practice structure and give you a quote for the full deployment.
Is this HIPAA compliant?+
Rico Cloud can be configured for HIPAA compliance at the platform level. The Medical Office system's automated messaging is designed as general appointment reminders and health information — not PHI transmission. Specific compliance requirements should be discussed on your discovery call so we can configure the system appropriately for your practice.
How does the annual recall system work?+
The recall system uses a date-based trigger configured during intake. When the trigger fires (e.g., 11 months after the last visit), the patient is automatically enrolled in a 3-touch recall sequence — one SMS and two emails spread over 2 weeks. If they don't rebook, they enter the 360-day long-term nurture sequence.
What platforms do the review requests go to?+
Google Business Profile is the primary platform — it has the highest SEO impact. Healthgrades is also included for medical practices where it's relevant. We configure your specific review links during the intake process.
My specialty isn't in the list. Can you still build this for me?+
Yes. Book a discovery call and we'll scope a custom medical specialty deployment for your practice. The core system architecture is the same — specialty-specific configuration is done during the intake process. Most specialties can be accommodated at the same price point.
