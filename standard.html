Common Questions

Everything you
need to know.

Which social platforms are supported?+
Standard includes Facebook and Instagram scheduling. You can connect multiple Facebook pages and Instagram business accounts. Posts can be scheduled individually or in bulk using the content calendar.
Can I manage multiple business accounts?+
Yes. Multi-account social is included in Standard. If you manage social for multiple locations or brand pages, you can connect them all and schedule posts across accounts from one dashboard.
Does this replace Hootsuite or Buffer completely?+
For most small to mid-size businesses, yes. You get scheduling, a content calendar, and post performance tracking for Facebook and Instagram. If you need LinkedIn or Twitter/X scheduling, those aren't currently included in Standard.
What's the difference between Standard and Core?+
Standard adds social media management on top of Basic. Core ($100/mo) adds automation workflows, 2-way SMS messaging, call tracking, missed-call text-back, reputation management, and a web chat widget. Core is where your business starts responding automatically — without you having to do anything manually.
Is there a limit on how many posts I can schedule?+
No limits on scheduled posts. Plan a full month, quarter, or longer — the content calendar and scheduler handle it all.
