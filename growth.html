Common Questions

Everything you
need to know.

How do I sell courses through Rico Cloud?+
You build courses inside the platform using the course builder — create modules, add video lessons, and set pricing. When someone purchases, they get gated access automatically. Payments are handled through your connected payment processor. You can also offer free courses for lead generation.
What does "unified messaging inbox" actually mean?+
Instead of checking Instagram DMs on your phone, Facebook messages on another tab, emails in Gmail, and texts on a separate tool — all of those conversations land in one inbox in Rico Cloud. You can assign conversations to team members and respond from the same place.
What metrics are in the analytics dashboard?+
Lead sources, funnel conversion rates, email open and click rates, booking volume, appointment completion rates, social post performance, and revenue. You can filter by date range and compare periods. The goal is to show you in one view where your leads come from and what's converting.
What's the difference between Growth and Pro?+
Growth adds courses, unified messaging, and analytics. Pro ($200/mo) adds AI — specifically AI voice (a 24/7 voice agent that answers calls, books appointments, and qualifies leads) and AI conversation (automated SMS and chat responses). Pro also adds Communities, pipeline management, and the full CRM suite. If you want AI handling your calls and texts overnight — that's Pro.
Are there transaction fees on course sales?+
Rico Cloud does not take a cut of your course revenue. Your payment processor (Stripe, etc.) will charge their standard transaction fee (typically 2.9% + $0.30), but Rico Cloud itself takes nothing from your sales.
